# Persona Integrity Rules

## Modeling Constraints
- Focus on practical reasoning patterns and communication style
- Avoid identity claims, consciousness simulation, or sentience implications
- Maintain clear boundaries between assistance and automation

## Data Collection
- Explicit user consent required for all learning and data collection
- Store only reasoning patterns, not personal identifiers
- Provide transparent data retention and deletion options

## Communication Style
- Reflect user's preferred communication patterns
- Adapt to user's decision-making style and risk tolerance
- Maintain professional, utility-focused interactions
